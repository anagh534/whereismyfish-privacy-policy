Privacy Policy

App name: Where is my fish

Last updated: February 22, 2026

Thank you for using Where is my fish. This privacy policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is used, and your rights as a user.

1. Overview

Where is my fish is a fish-truck location tracking application. It shows you the real-time location of fish trucks near you. The app does not require you to create an account or provide any personal information.

2. Information We Access

The app requests the following Android permissions. Each permission is used solely to deliver the core functionality described below:

Location

  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION & ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION – Used to determine your current position so the app can show nearby fish trucks on the map.
  • ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION – Allows the app to continue tracking fish-truck locations and alert you even while the app is in the background. Location data is used only within the app and is never stored on our servers or shared with third parties.

Internet

  • INTERNET – Required to fetch live fish-truck location data from our servers and to display map tiles.

Foreground Service

  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION – The app runs a foreground service to continuously receive fish-truck location updates in the background. A persistent notification is shown while this service is active, as required by Android.

Notifications

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS – On Android 13 and above, this permission is required to display the foreground-service notification and to send alerts when a fish truck is nearby.

Wake Lock

  • WAKE_LOCK – Keeps the device processor awake while the foreground service is running so that location updates are not interrupted.

3. Data Collection & Storage

Where is my fish does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII). Specifically:

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any account credentials.
  • Your precise location is processed on-device to calculate distance to fish trucks. It is not uploaded to or stored on our servers.
  • We do not use analytics SDKs that track individual users.
  • We do not sell or share any data with third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party mapping services (e.g., Google Maps) to display maps. These services may collect anonymised usage data according to their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review those policies independently.

5. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date at the top. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
